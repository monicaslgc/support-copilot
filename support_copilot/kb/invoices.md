# Invoices and payments
Invoices (fatura) are available under Settings > Billing, issued on the 1st of each month.
Refunds (reembolso) for duplicate charges are processed in 5-7 business days after approval by finance.
To change the billing details or payment method, go to Settings > Billing > Payment method.
