# Service outage or platform unavailable
If the platform is down, slow or unavailable for your whole team, check the status page first: known incidents are posted there.
During an outage our engineering team works on it right away and we update affected customers every 30 minutes.
You do not need to report an incident that is already on the status page; we will contact you once the service is restored.
Keywords: indisponível, em baixo, parou, falha geral, avaria, estado do serviço, caído, panne
